# Limitations

This research layer strengthens evaluation discipline but does not convert a portfolio prototype into a validated enterprise product.

## Internal validity

- Fixture labels may contain ambiguity or author bias.
- Deterministic rules can overfit known cases even without statistical training.
- Small class counts can make aggregate metrics unstable.

## External validity

- Windows versions, enterprise GPOs, VPN clients, security agents, and proxy products vary widely.
- Frozen fixtures cannot represent the full timing behavior of live endpoints.
- Results from synthetic/frozen replay must not be presented as fleet-wide production effectiveness.

## Construct validity

- Proxy drift is a reliability/control construct, not a malware or compromise diagnosis.
- Proof tiers represent evidence sufficiency, not calibrated probability.
- Macro-F1 measures classification agreement with labels, not business impact.

## Operational limits

- No autonomous remediation claim.
- No formal audit opinion.
- No guarantee of zero false positives or false negatives.
- No claim that evaluation results generalize beyond the declared dataset.

A strong result should narrow uncertainty, not erase it.
